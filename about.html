About

Hi all! I'm an aspirant game designer. I love playing trumpet, sprinting, mushrooms and, of course, play (not necessary with videogames or board games). I love being entertained and, above all, to entertain. I seek for new experiences to undertake, to explore myself or share with others.

I'm a PhD student of the civil engineering department at Politecnico di Milano, after a master in Engineering Physics and a bachelor in Material Science and Nanotechnology Engineering, with some months spent in Music Engineering and High Performance Computing Engineering.

My research topics involve multi-sensor fusion and calibration of MEMS and sensors in general, leveraging on data assimilation and multi-fidelity techniques, where deep learning may be included.

I hope there is the possibility to use the discovered instruments as videogame tools and mechanics. I'm here trying to give at least 50% of me to change career in the near/mid-future.

I also co-developed Feed Me, Quack!, an experimental roguelike game created by a small team of four people.

I’m especially interested in developing serious games, advergames, and games that preserve and share people’s memories and experiences.
