About

Hi, I'm Lorenzo

Engineer, PhD student, game design student, trumpeter and mushroom hunter.

I'm an aspiring technical game designer. I love playing the trumpet, sprinting, mushrooms and, of course, playing, with or without videogames and board games. I love being entertained and, above all, entertaining. I'm always looking for new experiences, to explore myself or to share with others.

On 11 September 2026 I started a PhD in Civil Engineering at Politecnico di Milano (DICA), after a research internship in the same department. Before that I took an MSc in Engineering Physics and a BSc in Materials Science and Nanotechnology Engineering, with some months spent in Music Engineering and High Performance Computing Engineering.

My research is about multi-sensor fusion and calibration of MEMS and sensors in general, built on data assimilation and multi-fidelity techniques, with deep learning where it helps.

In parallel I'm in my first year of Game Design at Accademia Italiana dei Videogiochi, with the goal of becoming a technical designer. I've taken part in two game jams, as game programmer and game designer on The Weigh of Gold and as game programmer on Severance Package, and I co-developed Feed Me, Quack! with a team of four.

I hope to bring the instruments I meet in research into games as tools and mechanics. I'm especially interested in serious games, advergames, and games that preserve and share people's memories and experiences.

As a 4-valve trumpet player, I had the chance to play with Ibrahim Maalouf in Bursa and Milan.
