🕶️ A‑MYCO: Prototyping the Eyes of a Myco‑Robot

Authors: Chiara D'Amato, Edoardo Torre, Lorenzo Vergata
Context: MSc Engineering Physics – Digital System Design course project by Professor Salice, Politecnico di Milano

A‑MYCO is a portable, low‑cost device designed to help hikers and mushroom enthusiasts safely explore forests by providing real‑time mushroom detection and species classification. The system captures video frames with a camera; a fine‑tuned YOLO11‑l model detects the presence of mushrooms, and a FasterViT‑0 network classifies the species from a custom dataset of over 1,800 mushroom classes (MO1822). The device alerts the user via speech synthesis (Piper‑TTS) only when a mushroom is spotted, allowing them to enjoy the walk without distractions. The prototype was tested on a Raspberry Pi 400, demonstrating feasibility for edge deployment.

My contribution: I worked on the dataset creation pipeline (downloading and cleaning Mushroom Observer images, handling synonyms, data augmentation), model training and evaluation, and overall system integration.
