Digital System Design · Politecnico di Milano

A-MYCO: Prototyping the Eyes of a Myco-Robot

A portable, low-cost device that spots mushrooms on a hike and tells you the species. A fine-tuned YOLO11-l detects them, a FasterViT-0 classifies over 1,800 species (MO1822 dataset), and Piper-TTS speaks the result. Tested on a Raspberry Pi 400. I built the dataset pipeline, trained and evaluated the models, and integrated the system.

Current project

Condominium meeting minutes, automated

A CPU-only pipeline for an 8 GB edge device: it records a meeting, transcribes Italian speech with a fast ASR network, rewrites it with an on-device LLM, typesets the minutes in LaTeX and prints them, one isolated folder per meeting.
